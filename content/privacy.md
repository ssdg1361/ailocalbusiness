*Last updated: November 12, 2026*

This site, ailocalbusiness.biz, is run by Spencer Scott Digital LLC, a New Jersey company doing business as AI Local Business. This page explains what information we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

## What we collect

**Information you give us.** When you ask for a free presence check or contact us, we collect what you type in: usually your name, business name, email, phone number, website and any notes. The free check form is run by Tally (tally.so). If you call or email, we keep the record of that conversation.

**Information collected automatically.** Like most websites, we use Google Analytics to understand how people find and use the site: pages visited, how long you stayed, the type of device and browser you used, your approximate location (city level) and the site that sent you here. We also use Google Ads conversion tracking to see when an ad leads to a phone call or a form. These tools use cookies and similar technology. We do not collect your name or contact details this way.

## How we use it

- To reply to you and do the work you asked for, like your free presence check.
- To follow up about our services. You can tell us to stop at any time.
- To understand which pages and ads work so we can improve the site.

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with anyone except the service providers that help us run the site and the business (such as Google, Tally and our email and customer records tools), and only so they can do that job for us. We may also share information if the law requires it.

## Google account data

AI Local Business connects to Google services, such as Google Analytics and Google Search Console, using our own Google accounts and the accounts our clients give us access to. We use that data only to report on and improve the websites and marketing we manage. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone outside that work. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including its Limited Use requirements.

## Your choices

- **Cookies:** you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can also opt out of Google Analytics with Google's [browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
- **Ads personalization:** you can manage how Google uses your data for ads at [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/).
- **Your information:** email us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, and we'll take care of it.

## How long we keep it

We keep contact and project information for as long as we're working together and for a reasonable time after, for our records. Analytics data is kept according to our Google Analytics retention settings.

## Children

This site is meant for business owners. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
