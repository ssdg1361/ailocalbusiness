ChatGPT now lets businesses claim a profile, and I've had owners ask whether it's worth bothering with. It is. Just not for the reason most people assume.

**Claim it, because it's quick and it puts the facts in your hands. Don't expect it to move you up on its own.**

## What a ChatGPT business profile is

When someone asks ChatGPT for a roofer in Ridgewood or a bakery in Fair Lawn, it can show businesses as results. Business Hub is where the owner of a business claims it, verifies it, and manages what ChatGPT shows: your website, description, category, logo and photos.

It's the same idea as Google Business Profile, a few years earlier in its life.

## What it does for a local business

- **The facts come from you.** Your description, your website, your category. Not whatever ChatGPT pieced together from an old directory.
- **You get a blue check.** Once you pass OpenAI's ID check, your profile shows as verified.
- **You see how often you come up.** Business Hub shows how many times ChatGPT showed your business as a result in the last 28 days, how many people clicked through to your site, and the general topics you came up for.
- **Nobody else holds it.** If you don't claim it, someone else can try. Then you're the one filing a dispute.

## What it doesn't do

This is the part to be clear on. **OpenAI says claiming doesn't guarantee more visibility** and doesn't give you control over who ChatGPT recommends. It also says ads don't change ChatGPT's own answers.

So who gets named when someone asks "who's a good plumber near me?" still comes down to the usual:

- A complete, active Google Business Profile
- Steady, recent reviews
- The same name, address and phone everywhere you're listed
- A website that says plainly what you do and which towns you serve

The profile makes sure that when you come up, the details are right. The work above is what gets you to come up. I covered it in [how to get your business to show up on ChatGPT](/blog/how-to-get-your-business-on-chatgpt/).

## A few limits right now

- **Local businesses can only claim an existing listing,** and only in the U.S. If ChatGPT doesn't have one for you, there's nothing to claim yet. Here's [what to do if you can't find yours](/blog/cant-find-business-chatgpt-business-hub/).
- **The numbers can be blank.** A small business may not hit OpenAI's reporting threshold for a while. Blank doesn't mean zero.
- **You never see the questions.** You see topics, not what people actually typed.
- **The AI sales agent isn't for you yet.** OpenAI's business agent is limited to U.S. online stores and online service businesses, and it can't take bookings.

## Should you do it now?

Yes. It's early, most of your competitors haven't heard of it, and it's a short job. The two places people get stuck are finding a listing that won't come up and getting through verification.
