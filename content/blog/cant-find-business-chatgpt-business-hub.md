You opened Business Hub, typed your business name, and nothing came up. Or it came up with a **Contact support** button where the claim button should be. You're not alone. This is the most common snag local owners are hitting in the first days.

**Here's what each situation means and what to do.**

## First, search a few different ways

Before anything else, try:

- **Your exact legal name** as it appears on your state filing
- **Any old names** the business went by
- **Your name plus your town,** like "Smith Plumbing Glen Rock"
- **The name on your Google profile,** if it's different from the sign out front

Listings can sit under a slightly different name than the one you use every day.

## If your business isn't listed at all

For now, OpenAI only lets **local stores and restaurants claim a listing that already exists,** and only in the U.S. There's no "add my business" button for local businesses yet.

OpenAI hasn't said where its local listings come from. What it does say is that ChatGPT draws on other sources when it answers questions about a business. So the practical move is to make sure the places it can read about you are right:

- **Your Google Business Profile,** complete and accurate
- **Your main directory listings,** like Yelp, the BBB, and the ones for your trade
- **Your website,** with your name, address, phone and service towns stated plainly

Then check Business Hub again in a few weeks. This is early, and OpenAI may open it up further.

If you run an online business, like an online store or a service you deliver online, you may see an option to add it. That option depends on where you are and what kind of business you have.

## If the listing says Contact support

That means **the listing is locked.** Select **Contact support** to ask OpenAI for help. When you write in, include the step you were on, any error message, and a screenshot with personal details blacked out. That's what OpenAI asks for.

## If someone else already claimed it

It happens. Sometimes it's a former partner, an old marketing company, or just a mistake. Open the profile, select the **three-dot menu (•••)**, and choose **Dispute ownership.** You'll verify with Persona, OpenAI's ID check, that you're authorized to represent the business.

Two things to know:

- Disputing ownership doesn't change how visible you are in ChatGPT in the meantime.
- If a marketing company claimed it for you, ask them to add you as admin or hand it over. Don't let anyone hold the only key to your listing.

## If verification keeps failing

- **The phone or email on file is old.** Use the website domain option instead, or ask whoever runs your website to add the DNS record.
- **The ID check failed.** Make sure your business name, address and any registration or tax number match your official records exactly. If **Try again** is available, use it with the corrected details.
- **It's been pending a while.** OpenAI says to wait for its email before trying again. Starting over can slow you down.

## If you have more than one location

Each location is claimed on its own. Claiming one shop doesn't claim the other, so search for each one separately.
