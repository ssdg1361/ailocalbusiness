## Website Design {#website-design}

A website is still the single most valuable digital asset most local businesses own. Your Google Business Profile, your AI search visibility, your ad campaigns — they all point back to it. If the site is slow, dated, or built for the wrong audience, everything else underperforms.

We build small, fast sites for local service businesses, not 30-page agency rebuilds. The goal: a site that loads quickly on a phone in a customer's driveway, surfaces what you actually do, and tells Google and AI crawlers exactly who you are.

- **Custom builds for local service businesses:** designed around the customer journey of "search, click, call" rather than corporate-style multi-page sprawl
- **Schema markup baked in from day one:** LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ, and Review schema configured so search engines and AI models can read your site cleanly
- **Core Web Vitals performance:** fast load, mobile-first, accessible. The metrics Google actually uses to rank you in 2026
- **Tied into your Google Business Profile:** NAP consistency, schema, links, and tracking working together so the site amplifies your local presence rather than fighting it
- **Built for AI crawlers, not just Google:** clean semantic HTML, llms.txt-ready, content structured to be cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews

To be honest about scope: we're not a 12-person agency. We build sites that work hard for small operators. If you need a 50-page enterprise site, we're not your team. If you need a sharp, fast site that helps you book more local work, we are.

## Paid Ads: Google & Meta {#ads}

Ads work when the foundation works. If your website is slow or your Google profile is thin, ads pour paid clicks into a leaky bucket. So we usually fix the foundation first, then turn ads on when they'll actually pay for themselves.

- **Local Services Ads (Google Guaranteed):** the pay-per-call ads at the very top of Google for service businesses. Honest take: you can run these yourself, and we'll show you how. Where we earn our keep is the part that decides how often you show up and what you pay per lead: your Google profile, your reviews, and how fast you respond.
- **Google Search ads:** for getting more of the searches you already win, and showing up in towns where you don't rank yet. We manage these around one question: for every dollar in, what came back? We cut what doesn't work and put more behind what does.
- **Meta ads (Facebook & Instagram):** best for getting your name in front of homeowners in specific towns before they ever search. Seasonal pushes, before-and-after photos, neighborhood targeting. Cheap awareness that makes every other channel work a little better.

If ads aren't right for your business yet, we'll tell you that too. It's cheaper to hear it from us than from your credit card statement.
