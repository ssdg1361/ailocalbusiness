## 2. Technical & Formatting Protocols

### The Answer Independence Principle (AEO Extraction)
When drafting content for AI search engines, ensure all informational headings are immediately followed by a short, definitive, declarative sentence. Avoid introductory fluff or compound sentences in the first 25 words of a section. AI crawlers look for clean, direct semantic matches. Bullet points must lead with bolded terms followed by crisp, independent assertions.

### Semantic Geo-Signaling vs. Keyword Stuffing
Never list more than three towns in a single user-facing sentence. To signal broad geographic coverage to search engines and AI engines without looking like 2012-era link spam, inject the complete target zip/town list directly into the `LocalBusiness` or `Service` JSON-LD schema blocks. Let the raw code handle the machine signaling while keeping the prose clean, natural, and local for human readers.

### The 2026 AI Monetization Split
When addressing AI search monetization, clearly separate ChatGPT from Perplexity. ChatGPT utilizes paid sponsor slots at the bottom of responses, meaning small businesses can now use targeted local ad budgets to buy visibility. Perplexity remains a purely organic, subscription-driven model. Both platforms rely on organic authority and clean technical structures for their main conversational text recommendations.

*   **Claude executes:** Markdown file frontmatter formatting, Hugo template logic, HTML/CSS tweaks, and git deployment steps.

### Required Blog Post Output Format
When generating a new post, always format it with:
1. Suggested Title
2. Meta Description (Under 160 characters)
3. Target Keywords (5-8 high-intent local phrases)
4. FAQ Schema Content (3-4 question and answer pairs)
5. Suggested Tags and Categories
6. Full Post Body (Written in clean prose, referencing specific Bergen County towns).

"Bergen County SEO" isn't a single target market. It's a collection of distinct battlegrounds—Ridgewood, Paramus, Fair Lawn, and Hackensack all have their own search volumes and local competitors. ### Customers are changing how they search
A homeowner dealing with a dead furnace in Wyckoff doesn't just scroll through pages of blue links anymore. They open an app and ask: *"Who is a reliable HVAC contractor near me with good reviews?"* 

The search landscape split this year:
* **ChatGPT** now mixes targeted local ads at the bottom of its answers with organic recommendations.
* **Perplexity** focuses entirely on an ad-free, organic citation model.
* **Google AI Overviews** pull direct answers straight into the search results page.

If your business isn't optimized to feed these systems the structured data they demand, you are completely invisible to a massive slice of your local market. This is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), and almost none of your local competitors even know it exists yet.
